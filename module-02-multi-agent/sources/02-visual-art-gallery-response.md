---
title: Galleries Split on Disclosure Rules
outlet: Contemporary Curation Quarterly
published: 2026-04-02
url: https://example.org/ccq/disclosure-rules
domain: visual arts
---

Exhibiting institutions have not converged on a standard for disclosing
generative tools in a work's production. Of 30 major galleries reviewed, 11
require disclosure, 6 prohibit generative work outright, and 13 have no
written policy.

Curators who favor disclosure argue that provenance is part of the work.
Those opposed argue that no comparable rule exists for other digital tools,
and that a disclosure requirement singles out one technique among many.
