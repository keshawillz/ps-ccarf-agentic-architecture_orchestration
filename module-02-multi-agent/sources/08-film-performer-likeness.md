---
title: Likeness Terms Become Standard in Performer Contracts
outlet: Screen Labor Weekly
published: 2026-05-06
url: https://example.org/screen-labor/likeness-terms
domain: film production
---

Digital likeness terms now appear in the large majority of scripted television
contracts reviewed for this report, up from a minority two years ago.

The terms typically require separate consent and separate compensation for
synthetic performance generated from a scanned likeness. Enforcement remains
the open question: performers' representatives say they have limited ability
to audit whether a scan was used, and no standard audit right has emerged.
