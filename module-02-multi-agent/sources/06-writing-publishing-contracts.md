Most major trade publishers now include a clause requiring authors to warrant
that a manuscript is substantially human-written. The clauses vary widely in
what they permit, with some allowing research and outlining assistance while
others prohibit any use.

Agents report that the vagueness is itself a problem: authors cannot tell
whether a grammar tool or a research assistant falls inside the prohibition,
and no publisher contacted for this piece would define a threshold.
