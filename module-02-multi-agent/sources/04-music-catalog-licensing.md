title: Catalog Owners Move to License Training Rights Separately
outlet: Rights and Royalties Digest
published: 2026-05-19
url: https://example.org/rrd/training-rights
domain: music
---

Several large catalog owners have begun carving training rights out of
standard licensing agreements and selling them as a separate line item.

Early deals reported in the trade press range from a flat annual fee to a
per-track structure. Publishers argue the split protects writers whose work
would otherwise be swept into a general digital rights clause. Critics note
that the artists themselves rarely control the catalog and may see little of
the new revenue.
