---
title: Generative Tools Reshape Commercial Illustration
outlet: Studio Practice Review
published: 2026-02-11
url: https://example.org/studio-practice/generative-illustration
domain: visual arts
---

Commercial illustrators report that generative image tools have absorbed much
of the low-margin work that used to fill their schedules: spot illustrations,
background plates, and first-round concept boards.

A survey of 412 working illustrators found that 61 percent had lost at least
one recurring client to in-house generative tooling since 2024. The same
survey found that 44 percent had raised their rates for the work they retained,
arguing that clients now come to them for the pieces that tools handle poorly.

Studios describe a barbell effect. Routine production work is drying up while
demand for senior art direction has grown, because someone has to decide
whether the generated output is any good.
