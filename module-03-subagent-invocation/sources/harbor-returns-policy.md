[page 1]
Customers may return any item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Items must be in original packaging. Opened headphones are accepted if the
hygiene seal is intact.

[page 2]
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of
the returned item arriving at the warehouse. Partial refunds are permitted
for items returned after 30 days but within 60 days, at 70 percent of the
purchase price.

[page 3]
Authorization holds are not charges. A hold placed at checkout is released
automatically within 3 business days if the order does not ship. Support
agents must not issue refunds against a voided authorization hold.

[page 4]
Refunds above 500 dollars require supervisor approval before processing.
Price matching against competitor listings is not covered by this policy.
