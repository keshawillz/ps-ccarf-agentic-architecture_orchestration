Rates for freelance commercial writing have split along a clear line. Generic
product descriptions, SEO filler, and templated email copy have collapsed in
price, with some marketplaces showing declines above 50 percent since 2024.

Reported, interview-based, and technically specialized writing has held steady
or risen. Editors interviewed for this piece said the deciding factor is
whether the work requires access to something a tool cannot reach: a source,
a lab, a product team.
