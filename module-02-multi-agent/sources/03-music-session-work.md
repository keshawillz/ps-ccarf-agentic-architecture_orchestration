title: Session Musicians Face a Narrower Market
outlet: The Session Report
published: 2026-01-28
url: https://example.org/session-report/narrower-market
domain: music
---

Demand for session string and horn players in advertising work fell sharply
between 2024 and 2026, with three of the largest ad-music houses reporting
declines above 30 percent in booked session hours.

Producers cite generative and sample-library tools that produce usable beds
for temp tracks and, increasingly, for final delivery. Session players
counter that the tools still fail on anything requiring interpretation, and
that the losses are concentrated in the most formulaic categories.

The American Federation of Musicians has opened negotiations over synthetic
performance credits in advertising contracts.
