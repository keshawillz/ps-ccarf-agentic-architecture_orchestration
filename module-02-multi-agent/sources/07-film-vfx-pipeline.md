title: VFX Houses Restructure Around Cleanup Work
outlet: Frame and Pipeline

Visual effects vendors have reorganized crews around correcting generative
output rather than producing shots from scratch. Two mid-size houses described
new roles whose entire function is detecting artifacts in generated plates.

Head counts have not fallen as sharply as predicted in 2024. Instead the mix
has shifted: fewer junior compositors, more mid-level artists whose job is
judgment about what looks wrong.
