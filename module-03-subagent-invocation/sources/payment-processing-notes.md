[page 1]
Charges post to the customer statement within 24 hours. Authorization holds
appear on the statement in the same format as posted charges, which is the
single most common source of duplicate charge reports.

[page 2]
A voided authorization and a posted charge for the same amount on the same
day will look identical to a customer reading their statement. Agents should
always check charge status before concluding that a duplicate exists.
